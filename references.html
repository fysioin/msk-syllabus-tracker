Full syllabus references

References & where to read them

Every reference named in the MPT‑MSK syllabus, per paper, alongside a topic‑by‑topic map to the exact textbook chapter. Each reference was checked against the publisher / catalogue record; corrections and current editions are noted.

verified as cited corrected / updated could not be verified
How this was checked. The topic→chapter mapping and the bibliography were independently re-verified against each book's table of contents and index, then corrected where the syllabus edition was superseded or a citation was inaccurate. Chapter titles are stable across editions — when studying an older edition, match by chapter title, not number.
Paper I

Fundamentals in Physiotherapy Practice, Pedagogy and Research

Principles of Physiotherapy Practice

  • World Physiotherapy - Standards of Physical Therapy Practice (policy document)
  • World Physiotherapy - Description of Physical Therapy (policy statement)

Core Professional Values & Research Ethics

  • World Physiotherapy - Standards of Physical Therapy Practice
  • ICMR National Ethical Guidelines (2017)
  • ICMR Policy on Research Integrity and Publication Ethics (2019)

Research Methodology and Biostatistics

  • Hulley et al. - Designing Clinical Research: study-design and biostatistics chapters
  • Indrayan & Malhotra - Medical Biostatistics: descriptive statistics; hypothesis testing

Exercise Physiology

  • McArdle, Katch & Katch - Exercise Physiology: energy transfer; nutrition; training adaptations
  • ACSM's Resource Manual - Ch 3 Exercise Physiology

Electrophysiology

  • Kimura - Electrodiagnosis in Diseases of Nerve and Muscle: electrical properties of nerve and muscle; peripheral nerve; EMG

Pedagogy in Physiotherapy Education

  • Singh, Gupta & Singh - Principles of Medical Education: learning theories; intended learning outcomes; assessment

Management, Entrepreneurship and Leadership

  • Page - Management in Physical Therapy Practices: managers and leaders; management responsibilities; fiscal/marketing (entrepreneurship)
Paper II

Fundamental Principles of Musculoskeletal Physiotherapy

Basic Concepts of Musculoskeletal System

  • Nordin & Frankel - Biomechanics of Bone, Articular Cartilage, Tendons & Ligaments, Skeletal Muscle
  • Levangie & Norkin - Ch 1-3 (biomechanical applications, joint design, muscle structure)

Basic Concepts of Anthropometry

  • ACSM's Resource Manual - Body Composition Status and Assessment

Basic Concepts of Biomechanics

  • Nordin & Frankel - Ch 1 Introduction to Biomechanics: Basic Terminology and Concepts
  • Brunnstrom's Clinical Kinesiology - Ch 1 Kinematics; Ch 2 Mechanical Principles: Kinetics
  • Levangie & Norkin - Ch 1 Biomechanical Applications to Joint Structure and Function

Biomechanics of Movement across Life Span

  • Nordin & Frankel - Ch 18 Biomechanics of Gait (CORRECTED: there is no general 'posture' chapter; Ch 19 covers indigenous postures)
  • Brunnstrom's Clinical Kinesiology - Ch 12 Stance and Gait (has the 'Developmental Aspects of Gait: Life Span Changes' section)

Physiological Basis of Human Movement

  • McArdle, Katch & Katch - Exercise Physiology: energy systems; neural control of movement
  • ACSM's Resource Manual - Ch 3 Exercise Physiology

Mechanics and Pathomechanics of Joints

  • Nordin & Frankel - Part 2 Biomechanics of Joints (knee, hip, foot/ankle, spine, shoulder, elbow, wrist/hand)
  • Levangie & Norkin - joint chapters (vertebral column, TMJ, shoulder, elbow, wrist/hand, hip, knee, ankle/foot)
  • Hertling & Kessler - Ch 3 Arthrology

Posture

  • Levangie & Norkin - Ch 13 Posture
  • Magee - Assessment of Posture
  • Brunnstrom's Clinical Kinesiology - Ch 12 Stance and Gait (stance posture and postural sway)

Gait and Function

  • Levangie & Norkin - Ch 14 Gait
  • Magee - Assessment of Gait
  • Nordin & Frankel - Ch 18 Biomechanics of Gait

Pain

  • Hertling & Kessler - Ch 4 Chronic Pain Management in the Adult
  • O'Sullivan & Schmitz - Chronic Pain chapter
  • Magee - Ch 1 Principles and Concepts (pain assessment, SINSS, referred pain)

Electro Physics

  • Kimura - Electrical Properties of Nerve and Muscle (note: no dedicated electrotherapy textbook is listed)
Paper III

Physical and Functional Diagnosis in Musculoskeletal Disorders

Assessment Fundamentals

  • Magee - Ch 1 Principles and Concepts (history, observation, scanning, joint play)

Regional Screening (clearing)

  • Magee - regional chapters (cervical, shoulder, lumbar, pelvis, knee, ankle/foot)
  • Hertling & Kessler - Cervicothoracic-Upper Limb Scan Examination; Lumbosacral-Lower Limb Scan Examination
  • Donatelli & Wooden - Ch 5 Screening for Referral

Clinical Decision-Making and Practice Models

  • O'Sullivan & Schmitz - Ch 1 Clinical Decision Making

Diagnosis and Investigations

  • Magee - special tests (CORRECTED: these are in the regional chapters, not Ch 1)
  • McKinnis - Fundamentals of Musculoskeletal Imaging (modalities and by region)
  • Greenspan & Beltran - Orthopaedic Imaging: A Practical Approach (imaging techniques; trauma)
  • Kimura - nerve conduction studies; EMG; entrapment syndromes
  • ACSM's Resource Manual - Exercise Testing section

Condition-Specific Assessment

  • Magee - Assessment of the Amputee
  • Hoppenfeld & Murthy - Bone Healing; Braces and Splints
  • Chui et al. - Orthotics and Prosthetics in Rehabilitation (amputation and prosthetics)
  • Ratliffe - Clinical Pediatric Physical Therapy (orthopedic disorders of childhood)
  • Saunders et al. - Hand and Upper Extremity Rehabilitation; Cooper - Fundamentals of Hand Therapy

Functional Assessment and Outcome Measures

  • O'Sullivan & Schmitz - Examination of Function
  • Cooper - Fundamentals of Hand Therapy: Assessment of Functional Outcomes
Paper IV

Physiotherapy Interventions in Musculoskeletal Disorders

Interventions for Physiologic Impairments

  • Donatelli & Wooden - Theory and Practice of Muscle Strengthening; Rehabilitation and Strength Training Concepts
  • O'Sullivan & Schmitz - Ch 10 Strategies to Improve Motor Function (closest fit; no chapter literally titled 'physiologic impairments')

Management of Pain

  • Hertling & Kessler - Ch 4 Chronic Pain Management in the Adult
  • O'Sullivan & Schmitz - Chronic Pain chapter

Methods of Musculoskeletal Rehabilitation

  • Hertling & Kessler - Functional Exercise (CORRECTED: Ch 5 is assessment/management concepts; technique chapters are Ch 6, 8, 9)
  • O'Sullivan & Schmitz - Ch 10-11 (motor and locomotor strategies)
  • Donatelli & Wooden - Defining the Core in Rehabilitation; Neuromuscular Rehabilitation

Advanced Techniques in MSK Rehabilitation (manual therapy)

  • Maitland's Vertebral Manipulation - The Maitland Concept
  • Maitland's Peripheral Manipulation - The Maitland Concept as a clinical framework
  • Kaltenborn - Manual Mobilization of the Joints (translatoric joint play)
  • Cyriax - Illustrated Manual of Orthopaedic Medicine (principles of diagnosis and treatment)
  • McKenzie & May - The Lumbar Spine; The Cervical and Thoracic Spine (classification; derangement; postural syndrome)
  • Travell, Simons & Simons - Myofascial Pain and Dysfunction (trigger point manual)
  • Butler - Mobilisation of the Nervous System (tension testing; treatment)
  • Shacklock - Clinical Neurodynamics
  • Chaitow - Muscle Energy Techniques
  • Cantu & Grodin - Myofascial Manipulation
  • Jones et al. - Jones Strain-Counterstrain
  • Mulligan - Manual Therapy: NAGs, SNAGs, MWMs
  • Grieve's Modern Manual Therapy (foundation and clinical sciences)

Electro Modalities in MSK Rehabilitation

  • No dedicated electrophysical-agents textbook is in the syllabus list (partial coverage: Hoppenfeld & Murthy - Modalities Used in the Treatment of Fractures)

Ergonomics

  • No dedicated ergonomics textbook is in the syllabus list (partial coverage: ACSM's Resource Manual - Occupational and Functional Assessments)

Trauma Rehabilitation

  • Hoppenfeld & Murthy - foundation chapters + regional fracture chapters
  • Clinical Orthopaedic Rehabilitation - region/injury rehab protocols
  • Maxey & Magnusson - Rehabilitation for the Postsurgical Orthopedic Patient
